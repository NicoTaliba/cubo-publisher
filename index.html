Cubo Publisher

Cubo Publisher publishes content created by Cubo to social platforms through the TikTok Content Posting API. The application authenticates the user's account with Login Kit and uploads videos to TikTok under explicit user authorization.

Automated, local-first content production assistant. No public user data is collected beyond what is required to publish authorized content.
