Privacy Policy — Cubo Publisher

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data we access

With your authorization via TikTok Login Kit, Cubo Publisher accesses only the data required to publish content on your behalf: basic account information (user.info.basic), your video list (video.list), and the ability to upload videos (video.publish).

2. How we use it

Solely to upload and manage the content you authorize. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

3. Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's own machine and used only to call the TikTok API on your behalf. They are not transmitted to third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account's "Manage app permissions" page. Tokens are then no longer usable by the application.
